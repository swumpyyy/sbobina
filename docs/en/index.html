For university students · Italian and English · free

From the recorded lecture to study notes, on your own computer.

Sbobina takes a lecture recording (a phone voice memo, a recorder, a Teams or Zoom file), or records from the microphone, and turns it into complete notes for studying and exam prep: a corrected transcript, outlines, a concept map, a glossary, review questions and a printable PDF. Everything runs on your Mac or PC. No account, no cost, and the audio never leaves your computer.

02 · How it works

Seven steps. Nothing made up.

An app that "records and writes" does one transcription and one summary. Sbobina checks its work several times: each step exists to remove one kind of mistake.

  1. Clean audio

    ffmpeg converts any format (m4a, mp3, wav, video…), removes echo and hum under the voice and lifts the parts where the teacher walks away from the microphone, without clipping the rest.

    → mono 16 kHz FLAC, the same for every engine

  2. Terms from the slides

    If you add slides, handouts or photos of the board, Sbobina pulls out technical terms, names and acronyms and gives them to the speech recognizer, so it spells them right. The course glossary grows by itself, lecture after lecture.

    → course glossary, editable in the app

  3. Transcription

    Whisper large-v3-turbo, local and free. For maximum accuracy you can add AssemblyAI and ElevenLabs: with two engines the audio is transcribed twice, independently.

    → words with their exact minute

  4. Transcript review

    The AI corrects the transcript block by block (terms, names, formulas spoken aloud, punctuation) using the glossary and materials. If two engines disagree, it picks the right version. It does not summarize or add: unclear passages stay marked as [incerto] (uncertain).

    → revised transcript

  5. Notes

    Written from the revised transcript and the slides. Anything not said in the lecture can only appear in a separate Integration box.

    → notes, map, outlines, glossary, questions

  6. Check and correction

    A second pass compares the notes with the transcript sentence by sentence: unsupported claims, wrong formulas or hypotheses, missing content, wrong diagrams. A third pass applies the fixes.

    → check report

  7. Deep dive

    Explains the topic beyond the lecture. What the teacher did not say sits in Integration boxes, and uncertain details are marked [da verificare] (to verify).

    → deep dive, expandable on request
Long lectures

On a Mac with an M chip, a one-hour lecture takes roughly half an hour to an hour for transcription and writing. Sbobina shows what it is doing live ("behind the scenes") with a time estimate. You can stop and resume: it picks up from the last saved block.

Queue

Add several recordings at once: they queue up and run one at a time. When a lecture is done you get a notification, with a sound if you want.

04 · Runs on your computer

The same app on Mac and Windows.

Same code, same interface, same features. Only the engine that runs the AI on the graphics card changes. On first launch a guided setup looks at your computer, suggests the right model and downloads everything once, with a progress bar.

Privacy

With the default settings nothing leaves your computer: audio, transcripts and notes stay in the data folder. Only if you add a cloud service key yourself is audio or text sent to that service.

Cost

Free: no account, no subscription, no cost per lecture. The price is time. Claude, AssemblyAI and ElevenLabs are optional and paid; with Claude the app shows the estimated cost of each lecture.

From source

Maczsh
# Python 3.10+ and ffmpeg, then:
git clone https://github.com/swumpyyy/sbobina.git
cd sbobina
python3 -m venv .venv
source .venv/bin/activate
pip install -e ".[locale,app]"
./scripts/crea_app_macos.sh

Creates Sbobina.app. With --dmg it builds a standalone app to share with classmates.

WindowsPowerShell
git clone https://github.com/swumpyyy/sbobina.git
cd sbobina
powershell -ExecutionPolicy Bypass `
  -File scripts\installa_windows.ps1

Installs Python 3.12, the packages and ffmpeg, and puts Sbobina on the Desktop. Needs a recent NVIDIA driver.

Recording tipsaccuracy starts here

Sit in the front rows or put the phone near the desk, microphone towards the teacher. Airplane mode while recording. One file per lecture. Add the slides: it is the most effective way to get terms, formulas and names right.

06 · Brand kit

Paper, ink, one accent.

The mark is the voice turning into text: five wave bars in a square of ink, the middle one in electric blue, like the sentence the teacher says is "on the exam". All 1-bit, like the paper the notes end up on.
